# Attribution and contribution

The circuit and operating method originate in **André Kesteloot, N4ICK, “Experimenting With Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum,” QEX, December 1986, pages 5–9**, published by ARRL. The correct supplied December issue supersedes the August issue initially included in the archive. Bibliographic details and its checksum are recorded in `qex-source.json`. The magazine itself is not included in this repository; [ARRL's QEX page](https://www.arrl.org/qex) identifies the publication.

Mohamed Abuain completed the university project individually: following the published design, building a Multisim implementation, assembling the breadboard hardware, observing its waveforms and spectra, and preparing the report. This is an implementation and experimental study of a published design. It is not a claim to have invented DSSS or independently originated the complete circuit.

The original article uses XOR/OR/NAND gates to compare the seven-bit words. Mohamed's report and schematic use a 74HC688 comparator. The report also documents a laboratory function generator as the 4 MHz reference. These are documented differences; the archived Multisim files have not been rerun here to establish every as-built change.

The KiCad files were a visual placement study. Mohamed explicitly confirmed that the components were not fully connected or tested. The current software extension, automated tests and documentation were developed later with AI assistance. Results generated by that extension are simulation results, separately identified from the original laboratory evidence.

Supervision acknowledged in the report: Prof. Tammam Benmusa. No additional individual contribution is inferred from file timestamps or software metadata.
